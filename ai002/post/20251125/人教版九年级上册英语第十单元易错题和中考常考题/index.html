Unit 10 You’re supposed to shake hands

一、单项选择易错题

考点 1：be supposed to 结构

1. — You ________ supposed to arrive after 8 o’clock.

— Sorry, I got up late this morning.

A. don’t B. won’t C. can’t D. aren’t

答案：D

解析： be supposed to do 的否定形式是 be not supposed to do，主语是 you，所以用 aren’t。

2. Students are supposed to ________ the blackboard before ________ the next class.

A. clean the chalk, began B. clean the chalk off, began

C. clean the chalk off, beginning D. clean the chalk off, begun

答案：C

解析： be supposed to 后接动词原形；before 是介词，后接动名词形式。

3. — Could you give me some advice on how to use my time?

— First of all, you ________ to spend more time studying.

A. supposed B. were supposed C. are supposing D. are supposed

答案：D

解析： be supposed to do 表示 “应该做某事”，用一般现在时。

4. You are ________ walk on the grass. It breaks the school rules.

A. supposed B. not supposed C. supposed to D. not supposed to

答案：D

解析： 否定形式需要完整表达 not supposed to，to 不能省略。

考点 2：情态动词表推测

5. — Is that Ms. Wang in the classroom?

— It ________ be her. She’s attending a meeting in Beijing now.

A. mustn’t B. can’t C. might D. could

答案：B

解析： can’t 表示 “不可能”，用于否定的肯定推测；mustn’t 表示 “禁止”，不符合语境。

6. — Where’s Tom? I can’t find him.

— He ________ be in the playground. He often plays basketball after school.

A. must B. might C. can’t D. needn’t

答案：B

解析： might 表示 “可能”，用于不确定的推测；must 过于肯定。

7. The light is on in the office. Mr. Smith ________ be in.

A. must B. can C. may D. might

答案：A

解析： must 表示 “一定”，有把握的肯定推测。

8. The news ________ be true. I can’t believe it.

A. can’t B. may not C. mustn’t D. needn’t

答案：A

解析： can’t 表示 “不可能”，对现在情况的否定推测。

考点 3：文化习俗相关词汇

9. We often drop ________ our friends’ homes after school.

A. for B. with C. by D. of

答案：C

解析： drop by 是固定短语，意为 “顺便拜访”。

10. — Tony is really a polite boy.

— Sure. He always ________ well.

A. behaves B. remains C. learns D. develops

答案：A

解析： behave well 表示 “表现得体”，符合礼貌的语境。

11. — What do you think of his speech?

— Oh, very good. It’s worth ________.

A. to listen to B. listening C. listen to D. listening to

答案：D

解析： be worth doing 是固定结构，listen 是不及物动词，需要加 to。

12. I think your suggestions will be ________ to me. I’m looking forward to ________ you soon.

A. value; hear from B. value; hearing from

C. valuable; hear from D. valuable; hearing from

答案：D

解析： be valuable to sb. 表示 “对某人有价值”；look forward to 后接动名词。

二、完形填空常考题

文化习俗差异

When you travel to a new country, it’s important to learn about its 1. customs. This will help you avoid misunderstandings and show respect to the local people.

In some Middle Eastern countries, like Egypt, people greet each other by 2. kissing on both cheeks. However, in India, people often put their hands together and nod their heads, saying “Namaste” (I honor you).

At the dinner table, customs vary too. In China, it’s polite to wait for the oldest person to start eating first. You should also avoid sticking your chopsticks 3. into the rice—this is considered bad luck. In Western countries, people use knives and forks, and it’s impolite to talk with your mouth 4. full.

Remember, when in Rome, do as the Romans do. By following local customs, you’ll have a more enjoyable trip.

1. A. languages B. customs C. foods D. places

2. A. touching B. kissing C. hugging D. shaking

3. A. into B. on C. above D. beside

4. A. full B. empty C. open D. closed

答案：1.B 2.B 3.A 4.A

三、阅读理解中考题

跨文化交际

阅读短文，回答问题

Dear Laura,

Thanks for your message. Yes, I’m having a great time on my student exchange program in France. I was a bit nervous before I arrived here, but there was no reason to be. My host family is really nice. They go out of their way to make me feel at home. The grandmother knows that I miss Chinese food a lot. So she actually learnt how to make Chinese food! She also has a teenage granddaughter about my age who is really kind. She always talks to me in French to help me practice. You wouldn’t believe how quickly my French has improved because of that. I’m very comfortable speaking French now. Although I still make a lot of mistakes, it doesn’t worry me as it used to.

My biggest challenge is learning how to behave at the dinner table. As you can imagine, things are very different from the way they are at home. For example, you’re not supposed to put your bread on your plate. You’re supposed to put it on the table! I thought that was pretty strange at first, but now I’m used to it. Another example is that you’re not supposed to eat anything with your hands except bread, not even fruit! You have to cut it and eat it with a fork. Another thing is that it is impolite to say you’re full. If you don’t want any more food, you should just say, “That was delicious.” Also, you’re not supposed to put your elbows on the table. I have to say, I find it difficult to remember everything, but I’m gradually getting used to things and don’t find them so strange any more.

I’ll write again soon and tell you more about my life in France. Hope you’re having a good school year.

Yours,

Li Yue

1. Why is Li Yue in France?

2. How does she feel about making mistakes when speaking French?

3. What is the biggest challenge she is facing?

4. Where are you supposed to put your bread in France?

5. Is it polite to say you’re full in France?

答案：

  1. She is on a student exchange program.

  2. It doesn’t worry her as it used to.

  3. Learning how to behave at the dinner table.

  4. On the table (not on the plate).

  5. No, it isn’t.

六、易错点总结

语法易错点

  1. be supposed to 结构

  • 易漏 to：正确形式是 be supposed to do，不是 be supposed do

  • 否定形式：be not supposed to do，not 不能省略

  • 时态变化：根据语境使用不同时态

  1. 情态动词表推测

  • mustn’t 表示 “禁止”，不是 “一定不”

  • can’t 用于否定推测，must 用于肯定推测

  • might/could 表示可能性较小的推测

  1. 固定搭配

  • drop by（顺便拜访）

  • be worth doing（值得做）

  • go out of one’s way（特地）

  • make an effort to do（努力做）

文化理解易错点

  1. 时间观念差异

  • 瑞士：非常重视准时

  • 哥伦比亚：时间观念宽松

  • 中国：对长辈要准时，对朋友较宽松

  1. 餐桌礼仪

  • 中国：不将筷子插在食物中

  • 法国：面包放在桌上，不是盘子里

  • 西方：不将手肘放在桌上

  1. 社交礼仪

  • 日本：鞠躬表示尊重

  • 法国：朋友间亲吻脸颊

  • 美国：初次见面握手
