Listening to music makes me feel relaxed and happy. When I’m feeling down, I always listen to music to cheer myself up. I also like to dance to fast music when I have spare time.

Music is very important in my life. It helps me express my feelings and understand the world better. I think everyone should have their favorite music.

题目二

When I’m in the mood for something exciting, I like action movies. I enjoy movies that have a lot of action and keep me on the edge of my seat. Spider-Man is one of my favorite action movies.

Sometimes I like to watch documentaries. I prefer documentaries that teach me something new about the world. March of the Penguins is a documentary that I really like.

📝 阅读理解

阅读材料一：About A Bing

A Bing was a famous Chinese musician. He was born in 1893 and died in 1950. He is known for his musical ability and his famous piece “Er Quan Ying Yue”.

A Bing had a very hard life. He became blind when he was young, but he didn’t give up. He learned to play the erhu and became a master of this instrument.

“Er Quan Ying Yue” is a beautiful piece of music that reflects A Bing’s sad life. When people listen to this music, they can sense a strong sadness and pain. But at the same time, they can also feel the beauty of life.

A Bing only recorded six pieces of music during his lifetime. It’s a pity that he couldn’t record more music. But his music has become an important part of Chinese culture.

阅读材料二：My Music Preferences

Different people have different music preferences. I like music that I can dance to. My favorite kind of music is pop music because it has great lyrics and wonderful rhythms. I prefer songs that I can sing along with.

My friend Tom likes classical music. He prefers music that is quiet and peaceful. He says that classical music helps him relax and think clearly.

My sister likes rock music. She enjoys music that is loud and energetic. She says that rock music makes her feel excited and alive.

What kind of music do you like? Do you prefer music that is fast or slow? Do you like music that has lyrics or instrumental music? Everyone should find the music that makes them happy.
